Requirements and opening the app

Packages target the systems below; installation has not yet been validated on every platform.

  • macOS: macOS 11.0 build target; universal DMG (Apple silicon and Intel). Open the DMG, drag the app to Applications, then open it.
  • Windows: x64, with Microsoft WebView2. Run the per-user installer, then open the app.
  • Linux: amd64 or arm64. Install the .deb with your package manager (GTK3 and WebKitGTK 4.1), or make the AppImage executable and open it on a compatible glibc distribution.

If Gatekeeper or SmartScreen blocks a file, stop and verify its source and release evidence; do not disable system protections. SHA-256 checks integrity, not the publisher's identity or notarization.

After installing

  1. Install and open the app.
  2. On first use, enter your own api_id and api_hash from my.telegram.org/apps in the form. The app saves both in the local vault (why?).
  3. Sign in by scanning the QR code with Telegram on your phone.
  4. Copy your AI client settings from the panel.

A new installation starts in read-only mode. Enable writing in the panel only if you want the AI to prepare messages for sending, always with a preview.
