Requirements and opening the app

This is a beta release: installation has not yet been validated on every combination of system and architecture.

  • macOS: macOS 11.0 build target; universal DMG (Apple silicon and Intel), signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a Gatekeeper warning. Open the DMG, drag the app to Applications, then open it.
  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, x64, with Microsoft WebView2. The installer is per-user and has no Authenticode signature, so SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”. Check the SHA-256 (below), click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  • Linux: Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, or Debian 13 or newer, on amd64 (x86_64) or arm64. Install the .deb with your package manager (it pulls in GTK3 and WebKitGTK 4.1), or make the AppImage executable and open it; the AppImage also runs on other glibc distributions at least as new.
  • Command line: telegram-mcp serve, without a window, works from the .deb, the AppImage and the .tar.gz archives.

If Gatekeeper blocks the DMG, or SmartScreen shows anything beyond the unknown-publisher warning, stop and verify where the file came from; do not disable system protections. SHA-256 checks integrity, not the publisher's identity or notarization; the minisign signature below shows that the checksum list came from the project.

After installing

  1. Install and open the app.
  2. On first use, enter your own api_id and api_hash from my.telegram.org/apps in the form. The app saves both in the local vault (why?).
  3. Sign in by scanning the QR code with Telegram on your phone.
  4. Copy your AI client settings from the panel.

A new installation starts in read-only mode. Enable writing in the panel only if you want the AI to prepare messages for sending, always with a preview.
